What We Do

More than JUST a weekly coding class

YMCA Code Academy helps students learn, practice, create, and share their work.

01

Free Coding Classes

Students learn Scratch and Python through free, in-person classes in the Paterson YMCA computer lab.

02

Online Lessons

Students can review presentations, study examples, and complete practice activities from home.

03

Code Challenge

Students build original projects for a free community coding competition.

04

Student Showcase

Selected young programmers share their approved projects with families and the community.

Our Story

From one coding class to a growing community program

YMCA Code Academy began as a free coding class led by a high school volunteer at the Paterson YMCA.

What started as a small group of students learning the basics of programming has grown into a larger effort to make coding education more accessible throughout the community.
