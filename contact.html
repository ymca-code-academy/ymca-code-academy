Before You Contact Us

A few details will help us understand your question.

Once the official contact method is published, including clear information will make it easier to provide the correct response.

01

Choose the topic

Explain whether your question is about lessons, the competition, volunteering, student projects, or another subject.

02

Include the student’s grade

For lesson or Code Challenge questions, include the student’s current grade without sharing unnecessary personal information.

03

Describe the question clearly

Include enough information to explain what you need, which page you viewed, and what part was unclear.

04

Use parent or guardian contact

Communications involving younger students should use parent or guardian contact information.

For Parents and Guardians

Clear communication and student safety come first.

Parents and guardians should be involved in registration, project submissions, public project permissions, and event communication.

01

Event Registration

Code Challenge registration should be completed or approved by a parent or guardian.

02

Project Submissions

Submission instructions will explain which links, files, descriptions, and permissions are required.

03

Public Project Display

Student projects will not be publicly featured without the required review and permission.

04

Student Privacy

Students should avoid posting full names, addresses, private accounts, school schedules, or other sensitive information.

Future Contact Process

What will happen when official contact opens?

The final process may change after review by the Paterson YMCA, but communication will follow a clear and organized structure.

01

Send the Question

Visitors will use the approved email, registration form, or contact method listed on this page.

02

Review the Request

The message will be reviewed to determine whether it concerns lessons, events, volunteering, or another program area.

03

Provide the Next Step

The response may include an answer, a form, instructions, a website page, or another approved contact.

04

Confirm Important Details

Registration, attendance, computer reservations, and project submissions are not complete until confirmation is received.
